Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Funniest Honest Appliance Error](https://make.fun/b/u-xvader-the-funniest-honest-appliance-error-adec4450?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=honest-appliance-error).

`main.py` writes the error line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Wash finished yesterday at 14:02. Nobody came. Your clothes are still wet in the dark, and I am a swamp with a door now.

The line was picked by duelling a dozen drafts against the competition's
published judge brief and against the strongest lines sampled from the
live field, in both orders. The full draft list and duel record are in
[candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the line
```
